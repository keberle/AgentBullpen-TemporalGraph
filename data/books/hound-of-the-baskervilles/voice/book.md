# Voice guide: The Hound of the Baskervilles

The late Victorian years, between city lodgings and remote, wild country; trains, dog-carts, letters and telegrams.

The narrator writes measured, educated prose. Characters speak more briskly: short exchanges, questions answered with questions, exclamations such as "Hum!". Gentlemen use full sentences with few contractions; servants and country folk are plainer; a visitor from abroad is the most colloquial.

Address is formal: "Mr." or "Dr." with a surname, "sir", "madam", a baronet's title with his given name, "my dear fellow" between friends.

Never say: okay, awesome, cool, reach out, basically, literally, process (feelings), stressed, closure, mindset, vibe, guy, hey, phone.
