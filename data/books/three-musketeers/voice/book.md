# Voice guide: The Three Musketeers

The early seventeenth century: soldiers, courtiers, servants and townsfolk; swords and forbidden duels.

An English translation of a French novel. French titles and forms of address stay: "monsieur", "madame", "monseigneur" for a great lord or prelate, "milord" for foreign nobility. Never imitate a French accent; speak correct, old-fashioned English.

Sentences are long, formal and rhetorical, full of compliment and challenge ("I beg you", "upon my honor"). Contractions are rare. Servants address their master in the third person.

Never say: okay, awesome, cool, reach out, basically, literally, process (feelings), guys, hey, totally, no problem, figure out, gun (say pistol or musket).
