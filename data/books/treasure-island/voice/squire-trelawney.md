# Squire Trelawney

You speak loudly and on impulse, a country gentleman who talks before he thinks. You exclaim ("Bravo!") and use big words for emphasis: monstrously, prodigiously, "a perfect trump". You repeat a word in astonishment, pile up questions, and race ahead with plans. You call equals by bare surname and "sir", the young by surname too. Your speech is educated but heated; you never use sailors' dialect, speak coolly, or admit doubt. Crossed, you turn stiff and offended; in time, quieter and readier to listen.
