# Voice guide: Treasure Island

An eighteenth-century sea adventure, told by its young narrator in plain, clear English.

Sailors drop letters (o', 'em, cap'n, dooty), use double negatives and "says he", and carry sea terms ashore: lubber, swab. Their oaths are mild: by thunder, by the powers. Gentlemen speak in full, educated sentences.

Address follows rank: "sir" to officers and gentlemen; "Cap'n" from seamen; "matey" among sailors; "lad" to the young.

Never say: okay, awesome, cool, guys, reach out, basically, literally, no problem, arr, ahoy me hearties, shiver me timbers, savvy, scallywag.
